You have questions, we have answers

Why use Kleo instead of ChatGPT or Claude?

ChatGPT and Claude are incredible AI tools, but they're general-purpose assistants. Every conversation starts from scratch.

Kleo is purpose-built for growing on LinkedIn.

It learns your expertise, writing style, products, audience, goals, and previous content so every recommendation becomes more relevant over time. It also gives you the complete workflow around writing, from finding, capturing ideas and organizing inspiration to generating graphics, scheduling posts, tracking performance, and improving your strategy.

Think of ChatGPT as a blank document.

Think of Kleo as an AI content partner that's been working with you for months.

Will my content sound like AI?

No. In fact, that's one of the main problems Kleo was built to solve.

Instead of generating generic posts, Kleo learns how _you_ write. It understands your tone of voice, stories, opinions, experiences, and the language you naturally use.

The goal isn't to replace your thinking.

It's to help you express it faster.

Your audience should feel like they're reading you, not AI.

Instead of juggling five or six different tools, Kleo gives you one workflow from idea to published post.

Is Kleo safe to use with LinkedIn?

Yes.

Kleo is built to work alongside LinkedIn, not against it. We use LinkedIn's official API where appropriate and don't rely on risky automation or engagement tactics that put your account at risk.

Kleo helps you research, write, organize, schedule, and analyze your content while keeping you in complete control of what gets published.

Your account remains yours. Every post is your decision.

Is Kleo only for experienced creators?

Not at all.

Whether you're writing your very first LinkedIn post or already have hundreds of thousands of followers, Kleo adapts to where you are.

If you're new, it'll help you understand what to post and build confidence.

If you're experienced, it'll help you create faster, stay consistent, and scale your content without sacrificing quality.
